About DP Pressure & Soft-Washing

Your local exterior restoration experts in Mandurah

Hey Mandurah locals and neighbors! If you're looking to refresh your home or business exteriors, DP Pressure and Soft Washing has you covered with our two expert services: high-pressure cleaning and softwashing. We serve Mandurah and the surrounding areas, delivering top-notch results every time.

Wondering about the difference?

High-pressure cleaning blasts away tough dirt, grime, mold, and buildup using powerful water jets – perfect for driveways, patios, and hard surfaces that can handle the intensity. It's like giving your property a deep, invigorating scrub!

On the other hand, softwashing is our gentler approach, using low-pressure water combined with eco-friendly cleaning solutions to safely tackle delicate areas like roofs, siding, and painted surfaces. It kills algae, mildew, and stains at the root without causing damage.

The best part? We often use softwashing as a post-treatment after high-pressure cleaning to maximize results – ensuring a thorough clean that lasts longer and protects your surfaces.
